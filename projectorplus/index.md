# Projector+ privacy policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

This policy covers Projector+, the app published on Google Play by the Projector+ developer. Projector+ controls the Insta-Share Projector, a Moto Mods accessory, from your phone. The public copy of this policy is at https://sexualmoose.github.io/app-privacy/projectorplus/ and the same text is built into the app.

## Summary

Projector+ does not collect, share or sell any personal or device data. It has no internet access, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

## Data the app uses

- Your projector settings (brightness, keystone, colour and similar options) are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone.
- The phone's battery broadcast is read to show the phone and projector battery levels and an estimated runtime. These readings stay in memory on the phone and are never saved or sent.
- The app talks to the projector through the phone's own Moto Mods service. Nothing from the projector leaves the phone.
- While the projector is on, the app notices when a phone call starts, so it can switch the projector off (the "Turn off during calls" option). It only learns that a call is in progress. It does not read phone numbers, contacts or call history.
- The app checks whether Motorola's own projector app is enabled, and reads three Android developer options that are known to interfere with the projector, so it can show a warning. It does not read the list of your other apps.

## Where data goes

Nowhere. Projector+ has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code, and it shares nothing with the developer or anyone else.

## Retention and deletion

Your settings are kept until you change them, clear the app's storage (Settings, Apps, Projector+, Storage, Clear storage) or uninstall the app. Clearing the storage or uninstalling deletes them. Android backup is switched off for the app, so they are not copied to a cloud backup. Nothing is stored on any server, so there is nothing to delete there.

## Permissions

- Moto Mods access and Mod control: turn the projector on and off, and set brightness, keystone, colour and picture processing, and read its temperature.
- Foreground service (connected device): keep the projector controls running while the projector is on.
- Notifications: quick controls while projecting, plus overheating and low-battery warnings.
- Display over other apps (optional): lets the projector's own settings button open the controls while another app is in front.
- Modify system settings (optional): only for "Lock to landscape", which rotates the phone while projecting and restores your rotation afterwards.
- Run at startup and wake lock: notice a projector that is already on after a restart, wake the screen when the projector's own settings button is pressed, and optionally keep the screen on while projecting.
- Change network state: Android 14 and later require it for the connected-device service type. The app never uses the network.

## Children

Projector+ is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the address above and in the app, with a new "Last updated" date.
