# WallPlanner privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

This policy covers the WallPlanner app for Android distributed on Google Play, published by the WallPlanner developer. The public copy is at https://sexualmoose.github.io/app-privacy/wallplanner/ and the app shows the same text under About > Privacy policy.

## Summary

WallPlanner works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access: the app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything you create in it anywhere. The app sends nothing about you, or about how you use it, to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app handles, and where it stays

- Wall photos: photos you take with the in-app camera. They are stored in the app's private storage on your device and processed there (straightening, wall and artwork detection with an on-device model, and layout).
- Pictures you add: images you choose in Android's photo picker. The app receives only the images you pick, not the rest of your photo library, and keeps a copy in its private storage.
- Project data: project names, wall and picture sizes, masks and saved layouts, kept as a database and image files in the app's private storage.
- Camera: used only while the capture screen is open, to take the wall photo. You can revoke the camera permission at any time in Android's settings.

None of this is collected by the developer, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or tracking.

## Permissions

The app asks for one permission, the camera, so that you can photograph your wall. It uses no location, contacts, microphone, storage or photo-library permission, and it has no internet permission.

## Exports and sharing

When you export a layout, the app saves a PNG image to your device's Pictures/WallPlanner folder. If you then tap Share, Android's share sheet sends that image to the app you choose. Both happen only when you ask for them. What the receiving app does with the image is governed by that app's own policy.

## Backups and device transfer

The app opts out of Google cloud backup. When you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, your projects (the project database and project images) can be copied directly to the new phone. They are never uploaded to the developer.

## Security

Your projects are kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps and protects with the phone's built-in storage encryption. The app has no server and no internet access, so no other copy of your data exists that would need protecting.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a project in the app (long-press it in the project list) removes its photos, masks, pictures and layouts. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes all of its data. Exported PNG files in Pictures/WallPlanner are ordinary images that you can delete with any gallery or file app. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request deletion of.

## Google Play diagnostics

The app has no crash reporting of its own. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google in your phone's settings, Android and Google Play can show app developers anonymous, aggregated crash and performance statistics. Google collects those under its own privacy policy; they contain none of your photos or projects.

## Children

WallPlanner is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the address above and shipped in an app update, with a new "Last updated" date.
